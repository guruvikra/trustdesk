Refund and Return Policy

Document ID: KB-REFUND-001 | Version: 2026.06

Key Grounding Clauses:

1. Physical Goods Window: Customers may report damaged, defective, or incorrect items within 30 days of the delivery date.

2. Damaged on Arrival: Physical items damaged in transit qualify immediately for a no-cost replacement order upon photo verification.

3. Software Licenses: Digital and software backup licenses are final sale and non-refundable.
